Total users always go up — even when your product is leaking. Cohort analysis stops the self-deception.

What a cohort is

Group everyone who signed up in the same week, then watch only that group over time. Repeat for the next week.

Reading the table

Rows are signup weeks, columns are weeks since signup. Strong products show warm colours extending right; weak ones fade fast.

Typical diagnosis

  • Week-1 drop-off → onboarding problem.
  • Week-4 collapse → value delivery problem.
  • Flat line after month two → healthy core.
