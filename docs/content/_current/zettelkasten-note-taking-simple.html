Most note systems collapse under their own weight because they optimise capture instead of retrieval.

Atomic notes

One idea per note, written in your own words. If you cannot summarise it, you have not understood it.

Links over folders

Folders force one classification. Links let ideas connect across domains — that is where originality comes from.

The daily practice

  • Capture, then rewrite before the day ends.
  • Add one link to an existing note — always.
  • Review a random old note weekly.
