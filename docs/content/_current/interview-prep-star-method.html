Interviews reward preparation that looks spontaneous. The trick is having a structure you can adapt under pressure.

STAR, used properly

Situation, Task, Action, Result. Most people spend 70% on context — flip it. Spend 70% on what you did and what changed.

Build a story bank

Prepare eight stories covering conflict, failure, leadership, ambiguity, learning, speed, quality and initiative. They answer ninety percent of behavioural questions.

Technical interviews

Think out loud, state constraints, start with the brute-force answer, then optimise. Silence is the only real failure.
