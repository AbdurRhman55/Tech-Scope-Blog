Focus is not a personality trait — it is an environment outcome. Stop trying to become disciplined and start designing defaults.

Timebox, then protect

Two 90-minute blocks with a single task each beat eight hours of vague availability. Put them in the calendar as meetings with yourself.

Make distraction expensive

  • Phone in another room — distance beats self-control.
  • Website blockers during blocks; it is not cheating.
  • Close chat apps; notifications are other people’s priorities.
You do not find time for deep work. You defend it, loudly.

End with a shutdown

Write tomorrow’s single priority before you stop. Your brain lets go when it trusts the plan.
