Scope creep rarely arrives as a demand. It arrives as a friendly extra that felt too small to refuse.

Email 1 — the welcome packet

Timeline, deliverables, communication cadence, and where requests should go. Warm tone, firm content.

Email 2 — the boundaries

What is included, what counts as a change request, and the response-time window. Send this before work starts, not after the first surprise.

Email 3 — the check-in

Midpoint summary of completed vs remaining items. Alignment is cheaper than renegotiation at the deadline.

Clarity is kindness. Vagueness is what actually damages relationships.
