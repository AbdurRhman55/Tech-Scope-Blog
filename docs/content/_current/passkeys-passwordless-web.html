Passwords failed because they asked humans to be machines. Passkeys succeed because they ask machines to be machines.

How a passkey works

A key pair is generated on your device. The private key never leaves; the public key lives on the server. Login is a cryptographic signature.

Rollout advice

  • Offer passkeys alongside passwords, never instead of them at first.
  • Make recovery obvious — people lose devices, not memories.
  • Remember cross-device sync is the whole usability story.

Phishing resistance comes for free: a passkey only works on the domain it was created for.
