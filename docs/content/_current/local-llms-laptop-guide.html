Local models crossed the “genuinely useful” line — for specific jobs. Expectations are still where the disappointment comes from.

What fits on your machine

With 16 GB of RAM, 7B–8B models at 4-bit quantisation run comfortably. Larger models need 32 GB or offloading tricks.

Where local wins

  • Private document summarisation — nothing leaves your device.
  • Offline drafting and rewriting.
  • Structured extraction and classification at scale.

Where it still loses

Complex multi-step reasoning and world knowledge. For those, frontier APIs remain worth the money.
