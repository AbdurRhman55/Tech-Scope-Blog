For tables, gradient boosting with thoughtful features consistently beats end-to-end deep networks. Here is why, and what to do about it.

Structure is a gift

Columns already carry meaning. The model just needs the right transformations: ratios, deltas, aggregations and time windows.

High-impact moves

  • Rolling averages over raw timestamps.
  • Per-entity aggregates joined back to the row.
  • Interaction terms that encode real-world logic.

Validate honestly

Random splits leak the future. Use time-based splits whenever your data has an order.
